## **Nivel A1.1: Greetings and personal information**

1. Multiple choice: Choose the correct greeting.  
   “It is 8:00 a.m.”  
   a) Good night  
   b) Good morning  
   c) Goodbye  
2. Fill in the blanks: Complete with am / is / are.  
   * I \_ Ana.  
   * He \_ my friend.  
   * We \_ from Colombia.  
3. True or False:  
   * “My name is Laura” is a way to introduce yourself.  
   * “I am fine, thanks” answers “How are you?”  
4. Matching: Match the question and the answer.  
   * What is your name? / Where are you from? / How old are you?  
   * I am 16\. / I am from Medellín. / My name is Sara.  
5. Multiple choice: Choose the correct pronoun.  
   “Carlos is my brother. \_ is 14.”  
   a) She  
   b) He  
   c) It  
6. Fill in the blanks: Numbers.  
   * One, two, three, \_, five  
   * Ten, eleven, \_, thirteen  
7. Order the words:  
   * name / is / My / David  
   * from / I / Colombia / am  
8. Choose the correct option:  
   * \_ are you? a) What b) How c) Where  
   * \_ old are you? a) Who b) How c) When  
9. Short writing: Write 4 sentences about yourself using:  
   * name  
   * age  
   * country  
   * favorite color  
10. Mini reading: Read and answer.  
    “Hello. My name is Emma. I am 12 years old. I am from Canada.”  
    * What is her name?  
    * How old is she?  
    * Where is she from?
